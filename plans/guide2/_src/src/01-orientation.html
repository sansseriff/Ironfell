Where things stand now

The first guide ended with a document store, a reconciler, bindings, clips and a model surface: one scene, drawn by Bevy, edited by drags and by Claude. Three days and 52 commits later, the same store holds units, definitions, constraints, compositions, ports and a session. Bevy draws every view into its own texture. The shell is a dock of tabs. This part is the map: what's new, how the stages fit together, where each system lives, and one interaction followed through all of it.

1.4One drag, through everything

The best single tour of the system is one gesture that touches every part: in the Schematic tab, drag the meter's reading bar wider. The laser's power slider moves, and both gauges, in both tabs, follow. Here is what happens, in order, with the part that explains each step.

  1. The shell forwards the press with its modifier bits; the dock lets it through to the canvas because the Schematic tab is a canvas view (Part 8). Rust knows the panel's rectangle and its pan and zoom (PanelView::affine, Part 5).
  2. Picking maps the pointer into the schematic composition and finds the reading bar. Its chain is just [reading]: the meter's schematic template has two roots (the block and the bar), and each copied root is a figure of the meter entity, sitting at the composition's top level (Part 5).
  3. The select tool applies Figma's rule: nothing encloses the bar, so the press selects it. Pressing the right edge of its transform box starts a resize (Part 7).
  4. The session records the selection as a selects relation from the session's selection node, with an id above 2⁴⁰, never saved and never in undo (Part 8). The meter's figures light up in the Optics tab too, because they picture the same entity (Part 5).
  5. The resize becomes an affine map, decomposed for a rect into a new size.w (Part 7), and previewed through the bar's handle (Part 3).
  6. The handle of size.w is linear: the width is bound to the meter's in.signal, which is derived from the fiber's out.signal through the connection (Part 9), which is the fiber's input times a constant, which is the laser's out.signal, which is params.power, which is bound to the slider. Inlining collapses all of that to a·slider + b, so the handle writes the slider's value (Part 3).
  7. The preview goes into the graph's overlay layer; the graph re-evaluates every dependent in one pass (Part 3). Both gauges, the beam's opacity and the slider's knob change.
  8. The bridge rewrites just the changed entities in the same frame (reconcile_gesture), and only the views that show them re-render their textures (Part 6). The Thermal tab, if visible, isn't re-rendered.
  9. On release, the gesture commits as one transaction, "resize rect", whose only op sets the slider's value. One Cmd+Z puts it back.

If the reading had come from a pair port (the heater's temperature bar), step 6 would stop: a physics-solved leaf is Locked, with the reason "solved by its across/through network; change a source or a parameter instead" (Part 9).

1.5New words

The design discussions fixed a vocabulary. These words are used precisely throughout:

definition / entityA kind of thing in the library, and one instance of it in the model2
templateA definition's drawing in one notation, copied to make figures2
handleHow an edit to a leaf lands: directly, through one linear source, or not at all (with a reason)3

presenterWhere a view sits: a tab today; later a popover or a menu8

1.6Running it yourself

Both crates are Bevy-free and test natively. The workspace defaults to wasm, so pass your Mac's target:

cargo test -p iron_document --target aarch64-apple-darwin cargo test -p iron_solve --target aarch64-apple-darwin

The app runs through the /run-iron skill, whose smoke.txt now has a section per stage: drags, the pendulum, Link, embeds, the select tool, the pen, the dock, and the optics and thermal chains. Its expected output is in .claude/skills/run-iron/SKILL.md. Two URL switches are useful when looking at the app: ?layout=default forgets the saved dock layout, and ?bevy=always turns render on demand off.

One known failure: iron_solve's three_link_chain_reaches_a_target hits its 50-iteration cap (Part 4's rough edge).

Before you go on
  • The store still owns everything. It now has layers of derived values (overlay, bindings, physics, clips) above the stored constants, and none of them is saved.
  • An edit to a leaf goes through its handle, which may land on a different leaf, through the solver, or nowhere, with a reason.
  • A composition is a diagram, an entity is a thing, and a figure links the two. Views of compositions can nest.
  • Every view renders to its own texture, only when what it shows changed.
