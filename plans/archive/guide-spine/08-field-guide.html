    Placing something that isn't listed

    Private helpers, tests and trait implementations like Display are left out. To place any other name, look at its file: the file-to-system table in Part 1 covers every file the change touched. Two rules of thumb: anything taking &mut Document is part of the op applier (Part 3), and any function whose parameters are Res, ResMut, Query or Commands is a Bevy system (Part 4).
