# The store's per-frame cost with one composition on screen, as a user
# working in a new tab has it: the demo's other pages hidden, which is the
# gate's worst case (handoff-patches.md PT-1.2, "Cost, as built"). Its
# `advance` once cost ~20 ms a frame here, at rest, dragging and playing,
# where an empty scope costs nothing. A drag must keep `advance` under
# 1 ms at p50, and the playhead looping 0-4 s under 5 ms: playing samples
# every clip, and the gallery's clips on hidden pages still evaluate their
# plots' rooted cells (`ROOTED`, replaced in PT-10), ~2-3 ms here. Then the Lorenz page
# alone, at rest: its "x against time" axis scrolls, so a tick label comes
# and goes every second or two (structure, which the gate decides again
# on); the worst frame's `advance` must stay under 12 ms (~25 ms when the
# gate walked every hidden composition three times, 2026-11-21). The whole
# demo, gallery included: `showcase=0` leaves the gallery out, and with it
# most of the document a user has.
