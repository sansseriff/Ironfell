# The schematic notation (plans/specs/schematic.md): the Circuit tab, a
# divider (V1 9 V, R1 1 kΩ, R2 = slider × 4 kΩ) built from the parts
# library and wired pin to pin, solved as one network. The probe reads 6 V;
# the slider down to a quarter makes R2 1 kΩ and the probe 4.5 V. R arms the
# resistor tool, Space turns it, a click places R3 on the grid; W arms the
# wire tool, and three clicks (R3's pin a, a corner, R2's pin b) wire them:
# one transaction, the corner tidied away. Dragging R3 moves it on the grid
# and its wire follows, the corner keeping both legs straight. Four undos
# put everything back. Then a probe P2 wired from its pin onto the top wire
# splits that wire at a junction (one transaction) and reads 9 V; Delete on
# R2 takes its two wires with it; a model's <circuit> edit draws a second
# divider in one transaction; the netlist reads it all.
