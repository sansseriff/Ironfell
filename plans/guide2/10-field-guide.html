    Placing something that isn't listed

    Private helpers, tests and trait implementations are left out. To place any other name, look at its file: the system table in Part 1 covers every file. Rules of thumb: a function taking &Document and returning ops is the store deciding what an edit means; one whose parameters are Res, ResMut, Query or Commands is a Bevy system; a pub fn in web_ffi.rs is called from TypeScript through the worker.
